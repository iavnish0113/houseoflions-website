About House of Lions

Good brands are built on good thinking.

The strongest communication begins long before the first piece of creative is made. It begins with understanding the business, finding the truth that sets it apart and shaping a story people can believe in.

We bring strategy, storytelling, creativity and integrated execution together to help ambitious brands move forward.

Insight before output.We earn the right to make things by understanding first.
Ideas before assets.A strong idea makes every format easier and every rupee go further.
Purpose before noise.Being heard matters more than being loud.

Our story

Experience and adrenaline. Sanity and bold moves.

House of Lions started with a simple frustration: too many brands were being sold activity when what they needed was clarity.

So we built a consultancy that thinks like a strategy firm and makes like a creative studio. Our work is always rooted in insight, and our stories are designed to last longer than the campaign.

Today the House brings together brand strategy, creative, PR and digital, with specialist teams in The Cub House, Mavcomm and TABB Solutions, working for clients from EV to café, beauty to real estate.
