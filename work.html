Property & PlaceHospitality Positioning Go-to-market

Turning a school-trip stop into a destination.

Monteria Village. India’s first sustainable village retreat had footfall from schools but little pull with weekend travellers and corporates. We repositioned it as a must-visit destination.

Monteria VillageAcres, near Mumbai36

  1. 01The challenge

    Spread over 36 acres near Mumbai, the retreat drew steady school groups but few weekend travellers or corporate off-sites. Its positioning gave the right audiences no reason to choose it.

  2. 02The insight

    Urban families and teams are not looking for another resort. They want a real reason to slow down and reconnect with where they come from.

  3. 03The idea

    A new positioning that placed Monteria firmly as a destination worth the drive, built around curated, traditional experiences.

  4. 04The execution

    Positioning statement, revamped go-to-market plan, brand manual, marketing templates, a new website and a refreshed digital presence.

  5. 05The outcome

    Results to be added with the client’s approval.

Beauty & WellnessBrand refresh Content Influencer Digital

Giving a crowded category a reason to choose.

Vibes Healthcare. With 31 clinics across India and Bangladesh, Vibes was competing on price in a cluttered market. We rebuilt how the brand shows up and speaks.

Vibes HealthcareClinics across India and Bangladesh31

  1. 01The challenge

    Beauty and wellness is a crowded market with few real differentiators, so price had become the deciding factor. Vibes was struggling to engage and to stand apart.

  2. 02The insight

    When every clinic promises the same results, people choose the one they trust. Trust is built by voices, not by discounts.

  3. 03The idea

    A refreshed brand with clear rules of engagement, led by real people: clients, experts and creators telling the Vibes story.

  4. 04The execution

    Brand refresh, go-to-market plan, rules of engagement, reels and short-form content, influencer campaigns, testimonials and a sustained digital marketing programme.

  5. 05The outcome

    Results to be added with the client’s approval.

Mobility & InnovationIdentity Brand guidelines Retail

One brand, from showroom to service bay.

Baxy Mobility. An industry leader in three-wheelers had hit a glass ceiling, held back by inconsistent branding. We gave it a refreshed identity and the system to hold it together.

Baxy MobilityWheels, one consistent brand3

  1. 01The challenge

    Baxy led its category on fuel efficiency and reliability, but inconsistent branding and no long-term brand roadmap were holding back its organic growth.

  2. 02The insight

    For a dealer network, consistency is credibility. A buyer should feel the same brand in the showroom, the service centre and the ad.

  3. 03The idea

    A refreshed identity designed as a system, so every touchpoint reinforces the same promise.

  4. 04The execution

    New colour scheme and typography, comprehensive brand guidelines, and a consistent rollout from showroom to service centre and from print to digital.

  5. 05The outcome

    The refreshed identity was well received across the network, and the client reported a positive effect on financial performance.
