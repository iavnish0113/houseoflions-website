Gen Z: let’s empower them

Generation Z must be prepared to shape the future as the world faces digital disruption, social change, and environmental urgency. Born between the mid-1990s and early 2010s, Gen Z is the most connected, informed, and diverse generation ever. Preparing them for leadership is essential.

Unlike previous generations, Gen Z has grown up in a world of the internet, smartphones, and instant access to information. Real-time events, social media, continuous exposure to climate change, mental health, inclusivity, and ethical governance shape their worldview. They understand these issues and are passionate about driving positive change. However, passion alone isn't enough; they need structured mentorship and guidance to lead in a challenging world.

Traditional command-and-control leadership models are rapidly becoming outdated. What is needed now are leaders who can inspire collaboration, embrace diverse thinking, and navigate complexity with agility and empathy. Gen Z can meet these leadership requirements, but they must be guided, coached, and prepared early.

Grooming Gen Z involves developing essential leadership skills, such as critical thinking, emotional intelligence, ethical decision-making, cross-cultural communication, and resilience.

Organisations investing in young leadership pipelines gain long-term advantages. Gen Z is values-driven, seeking purpose over profit and wanting to align with brands that reflect their beliefs. Empowering them early helps build authentic, inclusive, and future-ready cultures, ensuring smoother generational transitions and sustainable leadership.

Moreover, Gen Z’s digital-first mindset makes them natural problem-solvers in our tech-driven world. They are comfortable with change, open to innovation, and quickly adapt to new tools and platforms. These traits are vital in an era when technology is disrupting industries at an unprecedented pace.

India holds immense promise, with over 50% of its population under 25. If we nurture Gen Z talent effectively—through education reforms, mentorship programs, internships, and leadership accelerators—we can create a generation of changemakers, entrepreneurs, and visionaries who will define the future.
